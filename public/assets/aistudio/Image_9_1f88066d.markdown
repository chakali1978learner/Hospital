## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the confluence of clinical authority, empathetic patient care, and next-generation artificial intelligence tailored for the Indian healthcare ecosystem. The visual language conveys uncompromising diagnostic rigor balanced with human warmthâ€”delivering clarity under high-stress conditions while demystifying advanced AI-assisted treatment pathways.

The core visual style blends **Corporate Modern Clinicalism** with selective **Futuristic Medical Glassmorphism**. Structural foundations leverage pristine white surfaces, crisp clinical divisions, and high-contrast readability conforming to WCAG 2.1 AAA accessibility standards. Soft, diffused cyan-tinted glassmorphism overlays and luminous telemetry highlights are deployed intentionally for AI-generated insights, predictive diagnostics, and multi-specialty hybrid care monitoring. Visual clutter is eliminated to maintain calm confidence for both patients navigating appointments and clinicians reviewing critical vitals.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate atmosphere of institutional safety, scientific acumen, and digital precision:

- **Primary Clinical Blue (`#1769AA`):** Anchors core actions, structural headers, navigation frames, and high-importance institutional touchpoints.
- **Healthcare Teal (`#13A89E`):** Represents biological vitality and holistic recovery. Utilized for proactive wellness signals, successful appointment outcomes, and preventive care highlights.
- **AI Cyan Accent (`#0EA5E9`) & Vital Emerald (`#10B981`):** Applied strictly to algorithmic insights, real-time diagnostic streams, triage scores, and confirmed vital validations.
- **Neutral Dark Slate (`#243447`):** The primary text and high-contrast structural anchor, preventing the visual fatigue associated with pure carbon black while ensuring maximum legibility across all ambient lighting levels.
- **Backgrounds:** Foundation canvas rests on Light Clinical Mist (`#F5F9FC`), layered with Pure Optical White (`#FFFFFF`) containers and delicate Slate-Blue borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Emergency Crimson (`#DC3545`):** Reserved exclusively for urgent interventions, trauma triggers, critical lab threshold warnings, and immediate ambulance/tele-ER escalation controls. Never diluted with non-critical alerts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and structural titles, leveraging its geometric authority and soft humanistic terminals to engender warmth and confidence.

**Inter** handles all clinical documentation, medical charts, inputs, data values, and body narrative. Its micro-legibility under constrained screen resolutions guarantees accurate comprehension of critical dosages, lab figures, and bilingual descriptions.

### Bilingual Indian Context (English / Hindi)
All typography tiers must accommodate dual script rendering seamlessly. When Hindi (Devanagari) strings render alongside or within English components, line heights automatically scale by `+15%` to prevent diacritic clipping, while retaining matching visual optical weight against Inter and Plus Jakarta Sans.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8pt structural spatial grid, optimizing content density for data-dense clinician views while providing expansive, calming negative space across patient discovery flows.

### Grid Foundations
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Max-width container clamped at `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px â€“ 1199px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px â€“ 767px):** 4-column grid with `1rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Touch zones strictly enforce minimum 48px square tap targets.

### Breakpoints & Content Reflow
- Emergency actions (such as "Call Ambulance", "Instant Tele-Consult", and "Trauma Triage") pin persistently to the bottom viewport on mobile devices with high z-index stacking (`z-50`).
- Complex diagnostic comparisons reflow from side-by-side split view on desktop to progressive tabbed views on mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, favoring atmospheric clarity through clinical layers and controlled light reflection:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Light Background (`#F5F9FC`) flat surface with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient tinted shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(23, 105, 170, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(36, 52, 71, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive & Hover Cards):** Subtle lift using `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(23, 105, 170, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(19, 168, 158, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Sticky Navbars):** `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(36, 52, 71, 0.12)`.
- **Level AI (Futuristic Glassmorphic Layers):** Translucent backdrop blur layer for algorithmic diagnostic cards, AI prescription audits, and live vitals streaming:
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(12px) saturate(180%)`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.22)`
  - Inset Highlight: `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`
- **Level Critical (Emergency Notification Overlays):** `#DC3545` pulse glow with soft ring dissipation: `0 0 0 4px rgba(220, 53, 69, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary employs **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry:
- Standard interactive elements, input fields, badges, and alerts adopt an `8px` (`0.5rem`) radius.
- Cards, diagnostic modals, clinical record modules, and bottom sheets adopt a `16px` (`1rem`) radius (`rounded-lg`).
- Larger clinical dashboard containers and hero banner containers scale to `24px` (`1.5rem`) radius (`rounded-xl`).
- Pill shapes (`9999px`) are strictly reserved for bilingual indicator badges, operational status chips (e.g., "ICU Available"), and segmented tab selectors.
